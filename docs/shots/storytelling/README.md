# Storytelling rehearsal checkpoint

These five-size WebP captures show the shared family composition after Elof shrinks, and the finale's
Pappa shoulder ride with both carvings. They use public procedural rehearsal figures, not likeness
assets, and do not establish final art, animation or physical-device approval.

The fixtures use the real renderer and simulation. The opening fixture isolates successive saved
flags to inspect the picture; the finale fixture performs the eye-paint and home-ride actions in the
simulation. Ordinary keyboard opening and saved-story/choice flows are also checked by the broader
browser gate. The isolated pictures omit the gameplay HUD so actor framing can be inspected.

before-shrinking comparisons are written to the ignored `docs/shots/_work/` directories. Convert the
checkpoint images to WebP without preserving source metadata.
