## What a stranger notices in the first ten seconds

On a phone held sideways, first visit:

- **First.** A cream page with a small carved ghost bobbing, a paper bag of candy in its fists. Warm and clear.
- **Once the script runs.** The page turns flat grey-brown (#766a5a) under a cream card: the name in bold system type,
  "Måla Pappas nya träspöke.", a red "Börja" and the top of "Ny spelare". The ghost has gone behind the card. No sound.
- **When loading ends.** Behind the card, a dim, still, blurred floor shows through, with a candy counter "0", a pause
  button, a bird, a joystick ring, a dimmed hand button and a red "Hoppa".
- **"Börja".** It opens a second card, "Hur vill du spela?", with two stick figures. A tap there before loading has
  ended does nothing.
- **"Äventyr".** The screen goes black, with the counter, the pause button, the bird and "Just nu · Måla Pappas nya
  träspöke." floating on it. Then the kitchen fades up on Pappa's hands carving, with "Lördagsmorgon" on a scrap of
  paper: the first moment that looks like this game.

In ten seconds the ground changes four times (cream, grey, dim floor, black). There are two menus, 354 characters to
read before the first input, and silence until the scene. The best parts (the paper scrap, the hands, the tune)
come last.

## What already works and stays

- **The loading card** (`index.html:12-58`) is inline, shows before any script and makes no request. It stops
  bobbing for reduced motion, and its carved ghost holding the bag tells the premise in one picture.
- **The opening scene** needs no reading. It fades up on Pappa's hands carving and puts "Lördagsmorgon" on a scrap
  of paper at 0.9 s for 3.6 s. Bertil's hand creeps to the bag and Mamma's look stops it (`prolog-scenes.ts:26-83`,
  `phone/prolog-morning.png`).
- **One red start button** with a play icon has the focus when the title opens (`title.ts:44-56`), so Enter, Space
  and pad A start. Esc and pad B go back one step (`title.ts:180-186`).
- **The tutorial is wordless on screen.** It waits for 4 s (move) or 3 s (hop, act) of standing still, never shows
  during a held scene, and points at the real control wherever it is (`app/tutorial.ts:17-33`, `ui/tutorial.ts:24-31`).
- **Destructive confirmations** focus "✕ Nej, gå tillbaka" first, and names are set as text, never as markup
  (`title.ts:58-63, 74`).
- **Codes forgive mistakes.** Any order, small letters, commas, and å, ä and ö typed as a and o all work
  (`save/codes.ts:12-31`).
- **The name already has its right place in the story:** the title card over the garden as the ghost hops down
  (`prolog-scenes.ts:359-391`, `intro3/30-titel-title.png`). That is where Ori shows its name.
- **"Vänd skärmen på bredden!"** shows only on a touch screen held upright (`ui.css:1143-1147`).
