# UX audit: the menus and collections between play

> One of six audits of the UI, the UX and the presentation, made on 5 October 2026 at `0295eec`. Area: the pause
> panel and everything in it, the settings (also as opened from the title), *Jag har fastnat*, the album
> (stickers, *Hittegods*, *Små minnen*, *Pappas minnen*), the memory overlay, the photos and their viewer, Moa's
> map in the pause panel, *Utforska vidare*, the recovery messages, and how panels open, close and hand over
> focus. Line numbers are of that day.

I read the code that builds these surfaces (`src/ui/shell.ts`, `pause.ts`, `album.ts`, `memory.ts`, `photos.ts`,
`map.ts`, `explore.ts`, `title.ts`, `story-context.ts`, `ui.css`; `src/main.ts`; `src/input/input.ts`;
`src/save/settings.ts`; `src/content/sv.ts`) against plan §4.1, §4.5, §4.6 and §6.10 and the research note's §7. I
looked at the shared pictures `phone/` and `portrait/` `pause-top`, `pause-style`, `pause-graphics`, `pause-help`,
`pause-map`, `pause-album`, `pause-end`, plus `phone/memory` and `phone/explore`, and took eight of my own at tier
Low with stand-ins, under `ux-shots/menus/`: `title-settings-phone`, `stuck-ask-phone`, `controls-reference-phone`,
`album-found-portrait` (opened by tapping the bag), `photo-viewer-phone`, `byn-pause-end-phone`,
`message-load-failed-phone` (a 503 for the boot candy) and `explore-open-portrait`. The two frames in the photo
viewer were seeded into IndexedDB from a screenshot, so the HUD inside them is mine, not the game's. A script
(`scratchpad/menus-capture.mjs`, results in `menus-measure.json`) measured positions, boxes, computed fonts, focus
order and scroll heights at 844×390, 390×844 and 1180×820. Contrast ratios are computed from the CSS colours.

## What a stranger notices in the first ten seconds

A paper card over a warmly dimmed game, *Paus* and a big red *Spela vidare*: calm and clear. Then it turns into a
long web form. At 844×390 the first screen is that button and seven lines of story text; below it come 3,083 px of
content in a 366 px panel, 8.4 panel heights, most of it browser checkboxes with a word and no picture. What a
stuck child needs comes last: Moa's map 5.4 heights down, with no title and mostly blank paper, and *Jag har
fastnat* at the very bottom, 8.2 heights down. Once the panel is scrolled the ✕ has scrolled away with it. The
collections are better than their setting: the stickers are real pictures of the sweets, and the memory is a
sepia oval that grows from where it was touched. But a new album shows 24 question marks, the lost things are
words, and a photo is shown at 320×148 px inside a dark green letterbox.

## What already works and stays

- The paper card over a warm dim (`rgb(40 28 18 / .45)`), 22 px corners, and 64 px rows with 12 px gaps for the
  switches: big, calm targets.
- *Spela vidare* comes first, red with the play triangle, and is focused on opening. There are three ways out (the
  ✕, the backdrop, Esc or B), and Back first leaves the key reference or the stuck question (`pause.ts:121-127`).
- *Äventyr* and *Lugnt* are two drawn cards with one line each. These are the research's presets, and choosing one
  keeps the player's own switches (`settings.ts:64-71`).
- *Jag har fastnat* asks in pictures: a green ✓ with the big candy, ✕ with play. Nothing is lost, and the glitter
  carries Elof back (plan §4.5, as written).
- Settings, graphics included, take effect live while the game stays paused, without a reload.
- The bag, G and the pad's View open the album. A memory replays from its thumbnail and gives focus back to it.
- The stickers are pictures of the sweets. The memory oval is sepia cut paper that grows from its source, and
  becomes a fade under *Mindre rörelse*.
- The recovery texts are calm and true: "Spelet är pausat och det du har gjort är sparat."

## The inventory's defects on these surfaces

- **D2 holds.** On Byn, *Foton* and "Här samlas bilder från ditt äventyr." show while the album and the map are
  empty (`#albumPhotos` not hidden, `#pauseAlbum` and `#pauseMap` empty). The bag opens Pause at its top with focus
  on *Spela vidare*, because there is no album to scroll to (`menus/byn-pause-end-phone`).
- **D3 holds** for the volume − and + (48×48, 6 px from the value), the help levels (136×64 with 8 px gaps; 105×81
  in portrait), the *Lägg till på hemskärmen* summary (a 416×21 line) and the photo viewer at ≤500 px height
  (326×40). The graphics levels pass: they are 206×64 with 12 px gaps, because `.graphics` overrides
  `.levels` (`ui.css:1286`).
- **D4 holds** for *Helskärm*, the four *Grafik* buttons, the three *Hjälp* buttons, *Tangenter och handkontroll* and
  *Tillbaka till paus*. It is wider than listed: all ten switches are a word beside a checkbox, although
  `sv.ts:437` says every pause button "has a picture beside its word, for a child who can't read yet".
- **D1 holds on the recovery message:** *Försök igen* renders at 13.33 px, weight 400, in Arial, under 22 px text.
