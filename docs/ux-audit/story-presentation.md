# UX audit: story presentation

How the story is put on the screen, not what it says: the held scenes (letterbox bars, the HUD left at 28%, the
captions, the game's title over the garden), the time cards, the coda and the storybook page at a chapter's end,
the way from that page into the next chapter, the last shot and the credits, the four story panels (sharing, the
party, painting, carving), the memory overlay as storytelling, and how bubbles show who speaks and what they feel.
I read the code of `src/ui/scene.ts`, `cards.ts`, `prolog-scenes.ts`, `shell.ts`, `hud.ts`, `main.ts` (coda, page,
credits, title), `view.ts` (`snapshot`, the coda camera), `map.ts`, `memory.ts`, `story.ts`, `story-stroke.ts`,
`photos.ts`, `ending.ts`, `ui.css` and `sv.ts` at 0295eec, and the narrative audits so as not to repeat them. I
looked at the shared pictures (`prolog-morning`, `prolog-bubble`, `card-garden`, `end-page`, `end-tally`,
`memory`, `painting`, `title-saved`) at 844×390, 390×844, 1180×820 and 1440×900, and took eight of my own at
844×390 under `ux-shots/story-presentation/`: `title-card`, `prolog-page`, `coda`, `credits`, `ending-shot`,
`epilog-page` (tier High) and `party`, `carving` (tier Low). In the title, coda and ending runs I held the arrow
key, so those pictures show the keyboard layout (the hint line) instead of the touch controls. I also measured the
DOM in the running game (fonts, boxes, scroll heights, the map's text boxes) at 844×390, 390×844 and 1180×820, and
computed contrast ratios. The yardstick: Firewatch's and Monument Valley's cards, Unravel's photo album, Edith
Finch's text in the world, and Journey's and Gris's wordlessness (research note §1 and §3).

## What a stranger notices in the first ten seconds

- **Good:** warm near-black bars slide in, the picture comes up from black on Pappa carving, and a tilted scrap of
  paper says "Lördagsmorgon". It looks like a story starting.
- **Bad:** six controls stay over the film at 28%: the bag, pause, helper, the stick ring, Använd and Hoppa. The
  scrap is a 21 px system-font label in the middle of the shot.
- **Bad:** the first bubble, "MAMMA / Bertil! Godiset öppnar vi ikväll.", floats at the top centre. Nothing ties it
  to Mamma at the right edge.
- **Later, the same pattern:** the time cards, the coda and the page have good bones (paper, a glued photo, a
  crayon line) but are dressed as interface: system type, dialog cards, counters, and the HUD left on. Between
  chapters the main menu comes back.

## What already works and stays

- **The scene layer's grammar:** bars in `#120d09` that slide in over 0.6 s only while he is held, a fade from
  black, and a paper scrap for the time of day.
- **The page's order and source:**
  - its order: the photo, the name, the caption, Moa's map with the way on, and the tally last;
  - the photo is the coda's own last frame (`view.snapshot()`), with a white border, a −1.4° tilt and a shadow;
  - the red crayon draws the way on (1.8 s after 0.7 s), and stands still under Mindre rörelse.
- **The two-column page on a phone held sideways:** the picture, the name, the caption and *Nästa kapitel* are all
  in view (`tests/browser/endings.mjs` holds this).
- **The memories:**
  - the oval with sepia cut-outs;
  - one figure language: a flat cap is Pappa, a braid is Mamma, a spiky fringe is little Elof, a pointed cap is
    the first trägubbe;
  - the entrance grows from the shaving or the ghost, and a tap goes on.
- **The ending:** the wordless windowsill shot with its blink and bell; "Slut" as the last page's word; the credit
  to Pappa Emil's carving.
- **The story panels:** forgiving strokes with an assisted button ("✎ Måla med hjälp", "Tälj med Pappa"), and the
  arrow that draws the carving rule (always away from the body).
- **Bubbles:** at most 40 characters and one wordless babble each, and a held scene drops lines left waiting
  (`hud.hush`).
