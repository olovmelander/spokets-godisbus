# Access and devices: every hand, eye and device

One of six UX, UI and presentation audits made on 5 October 2026, at sg-work 0295eec. The auditor read `src/ui/ui.css`
whole, `shell.ts`, `hud.ts`, `pause.ts`, `title.ts`, `tutorial.ts`, `scene.ts`, `story-context.ts`, `input.ts`,
`main.ts`, `platform/offline.ts`, `sw.ts`, `vite.config.ts`, `index.html` and the built manifest. They looked at the shared
pictures in `ux-shots/{phone,portrait,ipad,desktop}` and took seven more, of which six are kept in
`ux-shots/access-and-devices/`: `s23-play.png`, `s23-end.png`, `desktop-title-focus.png`, `desktop-pause-focus.png`,
`iphone-bigtext-bubble.png` and `desktop-keys-verb.png`. Most numbers come from scripts with no picture, kept in
`scratchpad/aad/`. They read the box of every control and the gap to its nearest neighbour at 780×360 (the S23 held
sideways), 844×390 and 390×844, 1180×820 and 820×1180, 360×780 and 1440×900. Safe-area insets were emulated through
Chrome's `Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride`: 47/47/21 px for the iPhone held sideways and 47/34 px held upright. The
scripts also read:
- the computed fonts with and without *Större text*;
- the computed motion styles under no setting, under *Mindre rörelse* and under the system's reduced motion;
- style, layout and DOM writes per frame, from Chrome's performance counters and a MutationObserver;
- focus with the keyboard and with a stub gamepad;
- an offline reload with the service worker, and a history Back after a chapter change.

Contrast is the WCAG formula applied to the CSS colours, with opacity blended in. The dimmed Använd was sampled from
`phone/play-garden.png`. Physical sizes use 6.0 CSS px per mm for a 6.1-inch iPhone (460 ppi, 3×), 5.6 for the S23
(425 ppi, 3×) and 5.2 for a 10.9-inch iPad (264 ppi, 2×). On those, 2 cm is 121, 112 and 104 CSS px.

## What a stranger notices in the first ten seconds

- **Good.** A touch brings the controls at once, clear of the notch. The paper menus have big rows and dark-brown
  text on cream at 9 to 13:1. Nothing flashes.
- **Bad: the main buttons.** Hoppa is smaller than a child's thumb: 15.9 mm on the iPhone. Its word is a 13 px regular
  label in the browser's button font, white on red at 4.1:1. Använd, with nothing near, is a ghost at 1.8:1. On the
  S23 the helper's button sits 8 px above it.
- **Bad: other devices.** On a computer nothing says what E will do beside Pappa's hand, and focus is a thin browser
  line. On the Android phone, after 'Nästa kapitel', the system Back gesture throws Elof into the previous chapter
  and moves the save there too.

## What already works and stays

- **Safe areas.** Every corner button, action button, bubble, caption and panel uses `env(safe-area-inset-*)` with
  `viewport-fit=cover`. With the iPhone's insets emulated, the bag sits at x 59 and Hoppa 20 px inside the inset
  (measured).
- **The controls follow the hand in use.** A touch shows them. A key or a pad hides them and shows the key line
  (measured). No setting is needed.
- **Named dialogs and kept focus.** Every panel is a named modal dialog, and focus starts on its first choice ('Spela
  vidare', 'Börja'). Tab and the D-pad stay inside the open panel: Pause has 27 stops, measured. Escape and B go back
  one level, and focus returns where it was. Switches are 64 px rows whose whole label is the target.
- **Reduced motion and flashes.** The 3D view treats *Mindre rörelse* and the system setting alike, in the acting,
  water, helper, dressing, memories and painting. Nothing flashes: the aurora's light breathes over 7 s (0.14 Hz) and
  sways over 13 to 30 s, the dew glints are specks, and scene fades happen once, well under WCAG 2.3.1's three per
  second.
- **Ink on paper.** #3d2b1f is 9.4 to 12.9:1 on all seven paper colours. The time card is 10.7:1, and the purpose card
  is 11.3:1 even over black.
- **Offline and updates.** After one visit, a reload with the network off reached the title in 7 s (measured). All
  936 KB of packs are boot packs, precached. An update never interrupts a chapter.
- **A light DOM.** Plain play costs 2 style recalculations (0.24 ms) and no layout per frame (measured).
