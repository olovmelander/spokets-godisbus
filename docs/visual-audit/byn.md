# Visual audit: Byn, the village street and the sweet shop

> One of seven audits made on 5 October 2026: see [the overview](../visual-audit.md). The auditor looked at
> 20 to 30 pictures of the running game at 1180×820 and 844×390, on High and on Low, with stand-in figures.
> Names in brackets such as `b03` or `p17` are the auditor's working pictures, which are not kept. File names
> are as they were that morning: `src/render/dressing.ts` has since become `src/render/dressing/`, a module
> for each place. Line numbers are of that day.

## The first second

A stranger sees a boy on a dark strip in front of a bright, blurred, flat picture of a house: the street reads as a stage with a painted backcloth, and the bottom third of every picture is a near-black smear. What is already good and must be kept: the warm shop windows against a cool sky, the wares drawn as pictures instead of letters, the bicycle wheel as a great ring over him, and the shelves of giant jars filled with the kit's wrapped sweets. What lets it down most is that nothing behind him is a thing: the houses are 160×128 canvases enlarged ten times, unlit, squeezed to half their height, and more than half of each is above the picture. The place has room to be fixed: it draws 62 to 83 calls at 1180×820 and 94 at its heaviest (the start, on a phone), with stand-ins.
