# Visual audit: the pipeline, the light and the atmosphere

> One of seven audits made on 5 October 2026: see [the overview](../visual-audit.md). The auditor looked at
> 20 to 30 pictures of the running game at 1180×820 and 844×390, on High and on Low, with stand-in figures.
> Names in brackets such as `b03` or `p17` are the auditor's working pictures, which are not kept. File names
> are as they were that morning: `src/render/dressing.ts` has since become `src/render/dressing/`, a module
> for each place. Line numbers are of that day.

## The first second

A stranger sees a soft, layered storybook picture: painted far plates in each place's own palette, a sharp play plane, candy that pops, and nothing blown out. Keep that, together with Neutral tone mapping, the single grade pass switched by uniforms, and the rule that nothing compiles in play. What lets it down in the same second, in every chapter: the bottom third of the frame is the bank's front, smeared into vertical streaks and near black in five of eight places; nothing in the world casts a shadow, so a back-lit world looks evenly lit and things float; and nothing that grows moves. On a second look the three tiers are three different pictures (Low is the punchy one, Mid and High are milky, and the bog's water is dark green on Low and pale grey on High), the grade is one affine tint that cannot hold a curve, every edge is stair-stepped, and all the world's textures together weigh 4.5 MB beside 72 MB of render targets, with no normal map, no reflection and anisotropy 1 anywhere.

## Findings

Measured in this audit and used below (phone costs are reasoned from texture reads and passes, not measured on a phone):
- **Draw calls** at the same spot on Low / Mid / High: 94 / 96 / 101 (granskog 63), 67 / 69 / 74 (myren 65), 58 / 60 / 64 (garden 21), 62 / 64 / 68 (berget 108), 80 / 82 / 86 (norrsken 18), 68 / 70 / 75 (byn 66).
- **Resolution** at 1180×820 with a pixel ratio of 2: 1199×833, 1517×1054 and 1934×1344. A phone at 844×390 with a pixel ratio of 3 draws 1471×680 on Low and 1688×780 on both Mid and High.
- **GL memory:** 14 / 49 / 99 MB, of which render targets 0 / 30 / 72 MB (the High gate is 80) and every texture in the world 4.2 to 4.9 MB.
- **GPU time per frame** on this computer's integrated Radeon (timer query, relative only): about 1 to 2 ms on Low, 4.2 to 5.2 on Mid, 7.5 to 7.7 on High.
- **Programs:** granskog compiles 27 on Low and 33 on High, 17 of them `MeshStandardMaterial` variants of about 80 KB of GLSL each.
