# Visual audit: Gården and home (prolog, garden, epilog)

> One of seven audits made on 5 October 2026: see [the overview](../visual-audit.md). The auditor looked at
> 20 to 30 pictures of the running game at 1180×820 and 844×390, on High and on Low, with stand-in figures.
> Names in brackets such as `b03` or `p17` are the auditor's working pictures, which are not kept. File names
> are as they were that morning: `src/render/dressing.ts` has since become `src/render/dressing/`, a module
> for each place. Line numbers are of that day.

## The first second

On the deck and at home a stranger sees a striped salmon curtain (at home a beige or a mustard board) above a dark plank fence: the house is one flat unlit plane whose windows hang out of frame, and the bottom third of every picture is the ground's cut face. On the lawn the game suddenly has a picture: backlit grass, dew that twinkles, dandelion dots and birch trunks, sharp on the path and soft behind it; and under the deck the joists and the stripes of sun have mood. Those two must be kept. What lets the area down most is that nothing built or lying there is an object: the house, the boulder, the roots, the shavings, the hedge and the chapter's end are all the ground outline pushed 19 EL into depth and tinted, and beside yesterday's modelled candy they read as placeholders.
