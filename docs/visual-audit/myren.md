# Visual audit: Myren (the bog)

> One of seven audits made on 5 October 2026: see [the overview](../visual-audit.md). The auditor looked at
> 20 to 30 pictures of the running game at 1180×820 and 844×390, on High and on Low, with stand-in figures.
> Names in brackets such as `b03` or `p17` are the auditor's working pictures, which are not kept. File names
> are as they were that morning: `src/render/dressing.ts` has since become `src/render/dressing/`, a module
> for each place. Line numbers are of that day.

## The first second

A stranger sees a soft gold afternoon with a misty tree line, backlit straw sedge and bright candy. Under it stand blocks of brown-green cake in front of a pale, striped sheet, with a flat mustard rectangle below the waterline. The light, the sedge, the rust-green-gold moss tones and the candy's readability are already good and must be kept.

What lets it down most is the water. On High it is `#89826a` against the art bible's `#34423f`, it mirrors nothing, and its cut face shows the far scenery's ochre floor (`#6f541d`) over a third of a phone picture. On Low, where none of the effects run, the same water is dark (`#1c3322`) and the picture is better.

After the water come the shapes. The firm tussocks are extruded rectangles, the boardwalk is a garden deck 4.5 EL high and 17 EL deep, the dead pines are beige pylons, the shy tussocks are flying flowerpots and the crane is a grey potato. The mist whitens what is near while the far forest stays green.

## The five to do first

**1. Stop drawing what is not there (row 6).**
- **Built:** `visible` follows scale and opacity for the ride's crane, the helper figures, the call rings, the lantern, the bubble and sparks, the plane, the lace, the thought card and the glints.
- **Done when:** `p17` (x 180, 844×390, `light,chick,home`) is 90 calls or fewer (108 now), `06` is 57 or fewer (74 now), and `programs` is still 32 before and after a crane ride.
- **Could go wrong:** something shown for the first time compiles a shader. The start-up `renderer.compile` is meant to take hidden objects; the printed `programs` number proves it.

**2. The water (rows 1, 2, 10, 11).**
- **Order:** geometry (front at z 2.4, the back sheet, 60 EL back, 12 EL deep, one mesh); absorption and the cut face; the mirror and calmer ripples; the mist sheets after the water.
- **Done when, at x 57.6 on High:** the water between the tussocks is darker than the sky over the tree line; it shows the sky's gold and an upside-down dark tree line; under the waterline it is `#3a2a16` going to near black, with no ochre; mist lies in bands on the water.
- **Also done when:** from x 83.4, x 100.5 and the bough at (74.25, 2) no straight edge of water is in sight, and Low and High differ only in how much the mirror shows.
- **Could go wrong:** near things in the mirror (hence the 12 EL rule); blue and green candy over dark water at phone size (check `randig` blue at x 57); the family shots, which raise the camera but keep it level, so the mirror line stays at the middle.

**3. The tussocks and the bank (rows 3, 4).**
- **Order:** the UV fix (small, and the smear is gone at once); the island profile with lip, waterline and undercut; the baked strip; the normal map on Mid and High.
- **Done when, at x 12.7 and 42.7:** each firm tussock is a hummock with a rounded moss lip, a wet line and its foot in dark water; the bottom third has no vertical streaks; the face reads as peat with roots at 844×390 and stays darker than everything on the play plane (8–25% value), so candy over it still wins.
- **Could go wrong:** the play plane must stay level to the collision edge (stand him at x 11.4 and 14.0 with `at=`); the 8 EL repeat may show in a 16 EL wide phone picture (the corner tones and kit hummocks on the lip break it); the textures go in a `myren` pack, not the boot pack.
