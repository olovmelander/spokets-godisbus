# Visual audit: Berget and Norrsken

> One of seven audits made on 5 October 2026: see [the overview](../visual-audit.md). The auditor looked at
> 20 to 30 pictures of the running game at 1180×820 and 844×390, on High and on Low, with stand-in figures.
> Names in brackets such as `b03` or `p17` are the auditor's working pictures, which are not kept. File names
> are as they were that morning: `src/render/dressing.ts` has since become `src/render/dressing/`, a module
> for each place. Line numbers are of that day.

## The first second

A stranger sees a lovely sky (peach glow into blue-violet, violet ridges; at dusk a navy sky with a pink afterglow and stars) over a ground that does not belong to it: the bottom third of every picture is streaked brown or navy "fur", and on it lie brown loaves, white popcorn, grey bar tables and faceted eggs. Nothing reads as granite, and nothing says he is high up: no rock form, no cool shade against the warm sun, no shadow, no foreground, a ruler-straight edge where the plain stops. The place the whole story walks to, "den gamla tallen", is not there at all, and the northern lights at the climax are one flat mint wash. What is already good and must be kept: the two skies, the red rings on their sagging rope, the candy's colours in both lights, the glowing memory curl, and a budget with room in it (Berget 46 to 74 draw calls, Norrsken 65 to 99).

## The five to do first

**1. Rock that is rock (rows 1, 2, 3, 7, 13).** All in `dressing.ts`, no Blender needed for the first pass.
- *Order:* the mapping fix; `PROFILE_ROCK` with its chamfer, ledges and joints; walls subdivided and blocked, and the cut-back at a wall's top; a canvas `granite()` with the raised tones; the mountain's light. Then pictures. On Olov's computer the baked granite set replaces the canvas one.
- *Done when:* at berget 74 (b03) the bottom third shows a pale chamfered edge, two ledges and upright joints, with round grains and no streak anywhere; the granite's top measures about `#9a9ba6` and its shade side is blue-violet, not plum. At 143.5 (b12, b19) the cliff is stacked blocks and the lace with its two sweets is in full view from x 141. At 155.5 (b15), at berget 1 (b18) and at norrsken 2.4 (n01) there is no flat orange or black wall.
- *Could go wrong:* the drawn wall leaving the simulation's wall at the play plane (keep rows z −0.3 to 0.45 untouched; the robot cannot see this, so look with `?debug`). The mapping change shifts every other place's lip: take before-and-after pictures of garden, granskog, myren and byn. A normal map is a new shader variant and must be compiled in the first frames.
